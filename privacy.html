Privacy Policy for AppointMe Tracker

Last updated: August 12, 2026
Developer: Coffee Arcade Studios
App: AppointMe Tracker (iOS and Android)

This Privacy Policy explains how AppointMe Tracker (“AppointMe,” “the App”) handles information.

AppointMe is a local-first appointment, caseload, hours, and credential tracking app. Your client, appointment, credential, and caseload records are stored locally on your device. The App may also use third-party services for advertising and in-app purchases, as described below.

1. Summary

  • No AppointMe account is required to use the core App.
  • Client, appointment, credential, and caseload records are stored locally on your device.
  • AppointMe does not automatically upload your caseload records to Coffee Arcade Studios servers or a cloud synchronization service.
  • Core tracking features are designed to work without an internet connection.
  • AppointMe may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob.
  • Advertising services may process limited device, advertising, usage, interaction, diagnostic, and network information.
  • AppointMe does not send your client names, client contact information, appointment notes, credential records, or other caseload content to AdMob for advertising purposes.
  • Optional AppointMe Plus purchases may be processed through Apple or Google and managed using RevenueCat.
  • You control when your AppointMe records leave the device through backup, export, and sharing features.
  • Coffee Arcade Studios does not sell your client or caseload information. Your client and caseload information stays on your devices.

2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

AppointMe stores information that you enter or generate while using the App, which may include:

  • Client profiles, such as display name, client code, phone number, email address, address, notes, and status
  • Appointment information, such as dates, start and end times, duration, status, appointment type, location, notes, attendees, and recurring-series information
  • Credential or certification tracking information, such as credential name, required hours, previously earned hours, tracking dates, target dates, and progress
  • App profile information that you choose to provide, such as your display name, organization, and credential goal
  • App preferences, such as language, time format, hours display format, reminder settings, and theme
  • Local notification schedules used for appointment reminders, review reminders, and related App functions

This information is primarily stored in a local SQLite database on your device. Coffee Arcade Studios does not automatically upload this local caseload database to its own servers.

3. Advertising and Google AdMob

AppointMe may display banner advertisements through Google AdMob. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically process certain information when advertisements are requested or displayed. Depending on the device, operating system, region, consent choices, and advertising configuration, this may include:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate general location
  • Device or advertising identifiers
  • App launches and certain interactions with the App or advertisements
  • Information about advertisements shown or interacted with
  • Diagnostic and performance information, such as app launch performance, crashes, hangs, or related technical information
  • Other information needed for advertising, analytics, measurement, security, and fraud prevention

AppointMe does not provide AdMob with the contents of your local caseload records for ad targeting. This includes client names, addresses, phone numbers, email addresses, appointment notes, credential records, and similar professional records.

Advertising requires an internet connection. If the device is offline or an advertisement cannot be loaded, the core App continues to function without the advertisement.

Where required by applicable privacy laws or platform rules, AppointMe may display privacy or consent choices relating to advertising. Your available options may depend on your location and device settings. Google processes information collected through its advertising services according to its own privacy policies and terms.

4. AppointMe Plus and In-App Purchases

AppointMe may offer an optional one-time purchase called AppointMe Plus. AppointMe Plus may provide features such as:

  • Removing advertisements
  • Unlocking PDF report generation
  • Other clearly identified Plus features added in future versions

Purchases are processed through the applicable app store, such as Google Play or the Apple App Store. Coffee Arcade Studios does not directly receive or store your full payment-card information. AppointMe may use RevenueCat to manage purchase status and determine whether AppointMe Plus has been unlocked.

RevenueCat may process information related to purchases, such as:

  • Purchase history
  • Product purchased
  • Store and transaction information
  • Purchase or entitlement status
  • An App User ID used to associate the purchase with the App

When AppointMe does not require a user account, RevenueCat may use an automatically generated anonymous App User ID. AppointMe does not send your client, appointment, caseload, notes, or credential content to RevenueCat for purchase management. RevenueCat processes purchase-related information according to its own privacy policy and terms.

5. Information We Do Not Use for Advertising or Purchase Management

Coffee Arcade Studios does not intentionally send the following AppointMe record content to AdMob or RevenueCat:

  • Client names or client codes
  • Client phone numbers or email addresses
  • Client addresses
  • Client notes
  • Appointment notes
  • Appointment locations
  • Credential details entered by the user
  • Caseload history
  • Completed-hour records
  • Backup database contents
  • Exported spreadsheet or PDF contents

These records remain part of AppointMe's local application data unless you choose to export, back up, share, or otherwise move them from your device.

6. Permissions and Device Features

Depending on your device, operating system, and settings, AppointMe may use device features or permissions such as:

  • Notifications: to remind you about upcoming appointments, recurring series, backups, or appointments requiring review
  • File sharing or storage features: when you choose to create, save, share, export, or import reports and database backups
  • Phone features: when you choose to call a phone number stored in a client profile
  • Maps or location-opening features: when you choose to open an address or location that you entered
  • Internet access: for advertisements, privacy or consent services, in-app purchase verification, and related third-party services

These permissions and features are used to provide the functionality you request. They are not used by Coffee Arcade Studios to upload your caseload database to its servers.

7. Backups, Exports, PDFs, and Imports

AppointMe allows you to intentionally move information outside the App. Data may leave the App's local database when you choose to:

  • Create a database backup
  • Export an Excel spreadsheet
  • Generate or export a PDF report
  • Share or save a report or backup using your device's share sheet or file picker
  • Import a backup file that you select

When you export or share data, you control where that information is sent or saved.
