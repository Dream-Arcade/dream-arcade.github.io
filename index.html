Meet AppointMe Tracker.

A private appointment, caseload, and completed-hours tracker for people who need more than a basic calendar.

AppointMe Tracker helps counselors, trainees, coaches, tutors, and other professionals schedule sessions, record what happened, and keep a clear history of completed work.

  • ✓Schedule and organize appointments.
  • ✓Track clients, session status, and completed hours.
  • ✓Review reports, summaries, and credential progress.
  • ✓Keep app data stored locally on your device.
  • ✓Export and back up records when you choose.
