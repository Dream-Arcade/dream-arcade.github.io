Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common AppointMe Tracker questions.

Do I need an account to use AppointMe Tracker?

No. AppointMe Tracker is designed to work without requiring an AppointMe account.

Where is my AppointMe Tracker data stored?

AppointMe Tracker is designed around local, on-device storage rather than automatically uploading your app data to an AppointMe cloud account.

Can I track credential or professional hours?

Yes. You can use AppointMe Tracker to organize sessions and monitor progress toward credential, internship, supervision, or other professional hour goals.

Can I export my information?

AppointMe Tracker includes reporting and export features. Available options may depend on the version and features enabled in your app.

What should I do before changing or resetting my phone?

Create a backup from inside AppointMe Tracker and save it somewhere you control before deleting the app, resetting your device, or moving to a new device.

I found a bug or something is not working. What should I send?

Email us with a short description of the problem, your device model, operating system version, AppointMe Tracker version, and the steps that caused the issue. Please avoid sending sensitive client information.

How can I request a feature?

Send your idea to our support email. Feedback about features, usability, reports, and workflow improvements is welcome.

Where can I read the privacy policy?

You can read the AppointMe Tracker privacy policy at dream-arcade.github.io/privacy.html.

Still need help?

Contact Coffee Arcade Studios and include as much detail as you can about the issue. Please do not include confidential or sensitive client information in your email.
